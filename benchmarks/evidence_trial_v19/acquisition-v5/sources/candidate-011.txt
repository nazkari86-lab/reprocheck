# Results and interpretation

## Evaluation contract

- Dataset: public vEpiSet only.
- Test partition: 13 held-out subjects, 3,908 windows.
- Test prevalence: 11.11% (434 IED / 3,474 non-IED).
- Window length: 4 seconds.
- Every threshold below was selected on validation and frozen before test use.

| EEGPT temporal head | 25,589,507 | 0.8916 | 0.8641 | 0.6532 | 0.6139 | 0.6631 | 0.5714 | 0.9637 | 0.5440 |

The EEGPT linear probe achieved AUROC 0.7519, AUPRC 0.3722, and F1 0.3227. It
is included for reproducibility and ablation only.

EEG-DINO is the best individual ranking model. The four-model ensemble has the
best thresholded F1, precision, specificity, and false-positive rate in this
held-out split. The three-model EEG-DINO ensemble is the practical default when
avoiding EEGPT's additional memory and dependency cost.

## Calibration and uncertainty

For EEG-DINO Medium on the test set:

- Brier score: 0.0521
- Negative log-likelihood: 0.1891
- ECE (10 bins): 0.0530
- Subject-cluster bootstrap 95% CI for AUROC: 0.8513-0.9906
- Subject-cluster bootstrap 95% CI for AUPRC: 0.5949-0.9274
- Subject-cluster bootstrap 95% CI for F1: 0.5483-0.8169

The wide subject-clustered intervals are important: 13 test subjects are not a
large prospective validation cohort. Pooled window metrics should not be read
as certainty about a new hospital population.

## Interpretation limits

1. This is an internal held-out split of a single public dataset, not external
   multi-center or prospective validation.
2. Metrics describe four-second windows. Candidate grouping on continuous EEG
   changes the effective event-level operating characteristics.
3. False positives per minute are normalized using negative labeled windows;
   they are not a substitute for a full clinical reading-time study.
4. vEpiSet channel layout, annotation practice, hardware, patient mix, and IED
   prevalence may differ from a deployment site.
5. Thresholds may require a prospectively defined local validation protocol,
   but must never be tuned on a final evaluation cohort.
6. Automated outputs remain screening candidates requiring expert review.
